HOW TO PLAY
MOVE: Arrow/A-D or Left Stick — walk left/right.
JUMP: Space / JUMP button — press again in mid-air for a double jump. Also releases a rope swing.
ATTACK: J / HIT button — quick melee strike on nearby foes.
ROPE: K / ROPE button — fires an energy rope at the nearest glowing anchor ahead. While attached, steer left/right to swing, press JUMP to release momentum, or hold ROPE again to reel in and pull toward the anchor.
RESCUE: Some levels have trapped civilians (flashing icons) — walk into them to save them. Rooftop Inferno is timed — hurry!
BOSSES: Appear on Level 10 and Level 20. Watch for the flashing "telegraph" before a big attack, and use your rope to yank open their guard before striking the glowing weak point.
PAUSE: P or the II button, top right.
